# Providing Social Media Data to Political Candidates using R


Corey Stewart and Barbara Comsotck were political candidates for for US Congress from Virginia. I worked closely with  people in their campaign to create a weekly report on the candidate's performance on Twitter and Google.
Used Text Mining techniques on political tweets relating the the candidates and their opposition,incorporated data avialable on google trends.

I sought to offer insights on what people are saying about the race and identify twitter accounts that were the most vocal, either for the candidate or the opposition.
